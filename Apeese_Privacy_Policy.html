Privacy at a glance

Just the puzzle. Nothing else.

Apeese is an offline sliding-tile puzzle game developed by BITUMBUWA. Based on the app's intended design, it does not collect or transmit personal data, require an account, or ask for device permissions. Game progress can be saved locally on your device.

Offline gameplayNo loginNo trackingAges 13+

01. Information we collect

Apeese does not collect personal information or transmit gameplay information to BITUMBUWA or third parties. The game does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photographs, or other personal details.

The app is designed without advertising SDKs, analytics trackers, account services, or behavioral profiling. We do not sell user data.

02. Game progress stored on your device

To support gameplay, Apeese may save your current board, level progress, scores, personal bests, and related preferences using the device's local app storage. These records are used only to restore your game and track your achievements.

Local game data is not sent to BITUMBUWA or to an online server. It is not linked to a personal account.

03. Permissions and internet access

Apeese is designed to work entirely offline. Gameplay does not require an internet connection and the app does not ask you to grant access to sensitive device capabilities such as your camera, microphone, contacts, location, or files.

Standard operating-system functionality may be used to display the game and store progress locally. The app does not require you to create an account or sign in.

04. Data sharing and third-party services

BITUMBUWA does not share game data or personal information with advertisers, data brokers, analytics providers, or other third parties through Apeese. The game does not include third-party advertising or social-login integrations.

If you download the app through Google Play, Google may independently process information related to the store, download, installation, security, or billing under Google's own privacy terms. That processing is separate from the game's offline functionality.

05. Data security

Because Apeese does not transmit your gameplay records to our servers, there is no developer-operated database containing your game history or personal information. Local progress is maintained within the app's device storage, subject to your device's operating-system protections and settings.

No method of storage is completely risk-free, so we recommend keeping your device's software updated and using appropriate device security.

06. Data retention and deletion

BITUMBUWA does not retain personal or gameplay data on its servers. Locally stored progress remains on your device until it is cleared, removed through device settings, or deleted when the app is uninstalled, subject to operating-system backup and restore behavior.

To remove locally stored progress, you can clear Apeese's app storage in your device settings or uninstall the app. Clearing storage will reset your progress and scores. Because no Apeese account exists, there is no account to delete.

07. Age suitability

Apeese is intended for players aged 13 and above. It is a number-merging puzzle game and does not contain chat, user-generated content, social networking, or account registration.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from players of any age. The intended audience statement does not replace the age-group and content-rating declarations required by the app store.

08. Updates to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the app's features, data practices, or legal requirements change. When we do, the updated policy will show a new effective date on this page. Material changes to data practices will be disclosed as required before they take effect.
