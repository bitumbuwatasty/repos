We believe the next great idea can come from any village.

BITUMBUWA is the innovation and technology department of Elizabeth Foundation (ELFO). Our purpose is to equip young people in our communities with practical life skills, digital confidence and pathways to economic independence. From foundational ICT training to software development, we help young talent grow into people who create lasting value where they live.
